## General Questions

### What is GrepCut?

GrepCut is a professional, fully browser-based AI video editor. It allows you to create, edit, and export videos directly in your browser without the need to download or install any heavy software.

### Is GrepCut an online video editor?

Yes, GrepCut runs entirely in your web browser. This means you can start editing on any device with an internet connection, securely and efficiently.

### Do I need a powerful computer to run it?

Not necessarily! Because GrepCut relies on optimized web technologies and cloud rendering for heavy exports, you can achieve professional results even on standard laptops.

## Features & Editing

### How does Ai object tracking work?

Our Ai object tracking automatically follows objects or people frame-by-frame. You simply select the subject you want to mask, and the AI algorithm intelligently tracks its movement throughout the video, saving you hours of rotoscoping.

### Can I generate subtitles automatically?

Absolutely. GrepCut features an AI-powered transcription tool that accurately generates subtitles for your videos in just one click. You can also edit and style them to match your branding.

### What formats do you support for importing?

We support importing standard web video formats like MP4, WebM, and MOV, alongside images (JPG, PNG, WebP) and standard audio formats (MP3, WAV).
