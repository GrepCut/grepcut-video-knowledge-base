## Edit faster. Stay in flow. Make your time profitable again.

Manual editing is getting harder to justify. GrepCut handles the repetitive 90%, so you can focus on the creative 10% that moves the work forward.


## Plans

### Free (forever)

Start editing with ease.

- Browser editor for lighter projects
- No semantic scene search
- Generated Components in read-only mode
- No AI Usage included · 4K / 60 FPS export


### Pro (/ mo)

Create faster with AI.

- Everything in Free
- 5,000 semantic searches after indexing
- Custom Generated Components, AI agent, and BYOK
- $5 Gemini + $5 DeepSeek included · ≈11 h 40 min of indexing


### Pro Plus (/ mo)

Built for serious creators.

- Everything in Pro
- 25,000 semantic searches, 5x more than Pro
- Higher Custom Generated Components limits, AI agent, and BYOK
- $30 in AI Usage included · ≈35 h of indexing


### Ultra (/ mo)

Power for demanding creators.

- Everything in Pro Plus
- Unlimited semantic search after indexing
- Highest Custom Generated Components limits, AI agent, and BYOK
- $100 in AI Usage included · ≈116 h 40 min of indexing
