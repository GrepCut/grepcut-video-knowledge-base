## Why Your MP4 May Fail or Look Bad in WhatsApp

A phone or camera MP4 can be much larger than it looks. A short 4K clip, a high frame rate export, or an edit saved at a high bitrate can hit WhatsApp's practical sharing limits quickly.

The container name is not the whole story. WhatsApp tends to work best with broadly supported MP4 video, especially H.264 video with AAC audio, while unusual codecs or very large files may be rejected, reprocessed, or delivered with extra compression.

If you want the video to appear as normal chat media, you usually need a compact file. Sending as a document can preserve more quality in some cases, but it changes the recipient experience and may not show the same inline preview.

GrepCut is designed for the moment when you want a normal WhatsApp video message, not a cloud link and not a server-side upload.

## MP4 to WhatsApp Video Converter FAQ

### Can you compress an MP4 for WhatsApp without uploading it?

Yes. GrepCut processes your MP4 in your browser, so the source video is not uploaded to a conversion server. You choose the file, export the optimized copy, and keep both files on your own device.

### Will your WhatsApp video lose quality?

Some quality loss is normal when a large video must become much smaller. GrepCut aims to reduce bitrate in a way that still looks clear on mobile screens, but a huge size drop cannot be loss-free.

### Should you target 16 MB, 64 MB, or another WhatsApp video size?

Use the smallest target that still looks acceptable for your clip. WhatsApp limits and behavior can vary by platform, connection, and whether you send media or a document, so a compact MP4 is the safer choice for inline chat sharing.

### Why does your MP4 say video not supported in WhatsApp?

The .mp4 extension is only the container. If the video or audio inside uses an unusual codec, WhatsApp may reject it or reprocess it. Re-encoding to a WhatsApp-friendly MP4 profile can fix common compatibility problems.

### Can you compress a very long video for WhatsApp?

Yes, but you should expect trade-offs. If your clip is long, you may need to trim it first, lower the resolution, or accept stronger compression so the final file remains practical to send.

### Is there a file size limit in GrepCut?

GrepCut does not add a server upload limit because your video is processed locally. Your browser, device memory, battery, and available storage still determine how large a file is practical to convert.
