## How to Convert MP4 to PDF in Your Browser

1. **Choose your MP4**: Drop your MP4 file into the converter area, or select it from your device with the file picker.
2. **Choose how dense the PDF should be**: Decide how many frames you want to export. Use fewer pages for a quick storyboard, or more pages if you want a printable flipbook with smoother motion.
3. **Download your PDF**: Save the finished PDF to your device. Your MP4 is processed locally, so the source video does not need to leave your browser.

GrepCut uses browser media technology, including WebCodecs where available, plus custom PDF assembly. You control the frame count: the export can be a compact storyboard or a longer page-by-page flipbook.

## Use MP4 to PDF for a Storyboard or a Real Flipbook

An MP4 is meant to be watched. A PDF made from video frames is meant to be scanned, printed, annotated, or cut into pages. If you need a quick visual summary, you can export fewer frames and use the PDF as a storyboard.

If you want something more physical, you can export more frames and print them as a flipbook. When you cut, stack, and flip the pages one after another, the still frames create the feeling of animation in your hands.

That makes MP4 to PDF useful for edit reviews, classroom animation projects, client previews, stop-motion tests, or personal clips you want to turn into a small printed booklet.

If you need the same workflow for other video formats, use the [Video to PDF converter](/converters/video-to-pdf).

## How Many PDF Pages Should You Export?

There is no fixed 15-frame cap. You can export as many frames as you need for your storyboard or flipbook, depending on how detailed you want the result to be.

For a review document, fewer frames are usually easier to read. For a flipbook, more frames can make the motion feel smoother when you flip through the pages quickly.

Your practical limit is your device, browser memory, video length, and how many pages you actually want to print or handle. A very long PDF may be technically possible but awkward to bind, cut, or review.

### A simple rule: export for the way you will use it.

Choose fewer frames when you need clarity. Choose more frames when you need motion.

## What Your MP4 to PDF Export Contains

The PDF contains still frames extracted from your MP4. Each page or frame position represents a moment from the video timeline.

The export does not preserve audio, playback controls, subtitles, or the original video stream. It is a visual document, not an embedded playable MP4 inside a PDF.

For production delivery, legal review, or archival storage, keep the original MP4 beside the PDF. The PDF is best for viewing, printing, explaining, and marking up selected moments.

## MP4 to PDF Storyboard and Flipbook FAQ

### Can you convert an MP4 to a PDF without uploading it?

Yes. GrepCut processes your MP4 in your browser, so your source video stays on your device. You download the finished PDF locally.

### Can you make a printable flipbook from an MP4?

Yes. Export enough frames to show the motion, print the PDF, then cut, stack, and bind the pages in order. When you flip through the pages quickly, the still frames create an animation effect.

### Is there a 15 frame limit?

No. You can export as many frames as you want. For a short storyboard, choose fewer frames. For a smoother printed flipbook, choose more frames.

### How many frames should you use for a flipbook?

Use enough frames for the movement to read clearly when flipped by hand. A very sparse export feels jumpy, while a very dense export can become bulky to print, cut, and bind.

### Does the MP4 to PDF export include audio?

No. A PDF storyboard or flipbook contains still frames from your video. If you need dialogue, music, or exact timing, keep the original MP4 with the PDF.

### Is the PDF the same as an embedded playable video?

No. GrepCut creates a frame-based PDF. It does not embed the MP4 as a playable object inside the document.

### Can you use the PDF for client review?

Yes. Use a lighter storyboard export when the client needs to review shots, pacing, visual changes, or a screen recording without receiving the full video file.

### Is there a file size limit for MP4 to PDF?

GrepCut does not need a server upload, so there is no upload-size limit from GrepCut. Large videos and high frame counts can still be limited by your browser, RAM, and device performance.
