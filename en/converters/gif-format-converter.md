## Why Your GIF Can Be Bigger Than the Original Video

If your converted GIF is larger than the source video, that does not mean the converter failed. GIF is an older image animation format with a 256-color palette limit, while MP4 uses modern video compression that can store motion far more efficiently.

You can usually reduce GIF size by lowering the resolution, cutting the duration, or choosing a lower frame rate. A 6 to 12 FPS GIF can still look fine for UI demos, memes, reactions, and quick previews, while 24 FPS is better reserved for smoother motion when file size is less important.

Color is the other tradeoff. GIF cannot keep the full color range of a modern video or photo sequence, so gradients, shadows, and camera footage may show banding or dithering. For those cases, GIF to Video is often the better direction if your destination accepts MP4.

The practical rule is simple: use GIF for short loops and broad compatibility, and use MP4 when you need smaller files or smoother video playback.

## GIF Hub FAQ

### Can you convert a video to GIF without uploading it?

Yes. GIF Hub processes your video in your browser, so your file stays on your device. You can convert MP4, WebM, MKV, or MOV clips to GIF without sending the media to a server.

### Why is your GIF larger than your MP4?

MP4 uses modern video compression, while GIF stores animation as image frames with a limited color palette. To make a smaller GIF, reduce the resolution, shorten the duration, or lower the frame rate before downloading.

### Can you make a GIF from multiple images?

Yes. Use Images to GIF, add JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, or TIFF files, reorder the frames, and set the playback speed from the available controls.

### Can you turn a GIF into a video?

Yes. Use GIF to Video to convert an animated GIF into an MP4 file. This is useful when you want video playback controls or a smaller file for apps that handle MP4 better than GIF.

### Can you extract every frame from a GIF?

Yes. Use GIF to Frames and choose PNG for lossless frame images or JPG for smaller frame files. GIF Hub packages the extracted frames in one ZIP archive.

### Will your GIF keep all video colors?

No. GIF is limited to a maximum 256-color palette per frame. If your source has gradients, shadows, or camera footage, you may see color banding or dithering in the GIF output.

### What frame rate should you choose for a GIF?

Use a lower frame rate when file size matters, especially for short demos or simple loops. Use a higher frame rate when smooth motion matters more than download size.
