## Why Your WMV File May Need MP4

WMV is a Windows Media format. If your video came from an older Windows workflow, a saved webinar, a training recording, or an archived presentation, it may still play on one Windows computer but fail in another app.

That usually happens because the file is not just a video extension. A WMV file often contains Windows Media Video and Windows Media Audio inside an ASF container, and not every modern device or browser includes support for those codecs.

MP4 is a more practical format when you need your video to move between devices. Converting your WMV to MP4 gives you a file that is easier to open on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android, smart TVs, web pages, and common editing tools.

Renaming `video.wmv` to `video.mp4` will not solve playback problems. The media needs to be converted into an MP4 file that your target app can actually decode.

## What the WMV to MP4 Conversion Does

GrepCut does not just change the file extension. It reads the WMV video, decodes the frames your browser can process, and creates a new MP4 file using modern output settings.

The result is an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. That combination is widely used because it balances compatibility, quality, and file size for everyday playback and sharing.

Because WMV to MP4 is a transcode, it is not the same as a loss-free remux. GrepCut is designed to preserve your video clearly while making it more compatible, but any re-encoding step can slightly change the final file.

## WMV to MP4 Converter FAQ

### Can you convert WMV to MP4 without uploading the file?

Yes. GrepCut converts your WMV locally in your browser, so your video stays on your device. That is useful when your file contains private, personal, client, or business footage.

### Will your WMV file work on iPhone after conversion?

The MP4 output is designed for broad modern playback with H.264 video and AAC audio. That makes it a much better target for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, browsers, and sharing apps than a legacy WMV file.

### Can you just rename WMV to MP4?

No. Renaming only changes the file name. It does not change the container, video codec, or audio codec inside the file. If your app cannot play WMV, you need a real conversion.

### Will converting WMV to MP4 reduce quality?

WMV to MP4 conversion usually requires transcoding, so it is not loss-free in the strict technical sense. GrepCut uses modern H.264 and AAC output to keep the converted MP4 clear while improving compatibility.

### Why does your WMV play on Windows but not on Mac?

Your WMV may rely on Windows Media codecs that are available on one Windows setup but missing in your Mac app. Converting to MP4 gives you a more portable file that current Apple and browser workflows handle more reliably.

### Can you use the converted MP4 on a website?

Yes, MP4 is a common format for web video. For the best result, test the exported file in the browsers you care about, especially if you plan to embed it with an HTML5 `<video>` element.

### What if your WMV file is very large?

Large videos can take longer because your device performs the conversion locally. Keep the browser tab open until the MP4 is finished, and make sure your device has enough available memory.

### What if the WMV conversion fails?

The file may be corrupted, DRM-protected, incomplete, or encoded with an unusual legacy variant. If possible, try a shorter export from the original source or check whether the WMV plays correctly before conversion.
