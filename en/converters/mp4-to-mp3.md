## What Actually Changes During MP4 to MP3 Conversion

MP4 is a container, not a single audio format. Your MP4 may contain AAC or another audio track, while MP3 is its own compressed audio format. That means converting to MP3 is not just renaming the file extension.

GrepCut extracts the audio stream and re-encodes it as MP3 at 192 kbps. This is a practical setting for speech, lectures, interviews, and casual listening because it balances compatibility, file size, and sound quality.

If your source audio is already compressed, the MP3 is still a new encoded file. You should not expect a lossless copy, but you should get a broadly compatible audio file that is easy to use outside video apps.

### Why is the MP3 smaller than the MP4?
