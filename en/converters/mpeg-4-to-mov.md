## Why You Should Not Just Rename .mp4 to .mov

Renaming a file only changes the label at the end of the filename. It does not rewrite the video track, audio track, timing data, or container structure.

That shortcut can appear to work with some files, but it can also produce glitchy playback, import errors, or sync problems. A real MPEG-4 to MOV transcode rebuilds the output as a MOV file so your app receives the format you asked for.

## MPEG-4 to MOV Converter FAQ

### Can you convert MPEG-4 to MOV without uploading the file?

Yes. GrepCut processes your video in your browser, so your MPEG-4 file stays on your device and is not uploaded to a server.

### Will converting MPEG-4 to MOV make your video lossless?

No. This converter transcodes your video into H.264/AAC MOV, so a new encoded file is created. GrepCut uses high-quality settings to preserve visual detail, but this is not a lossless remux.

### Is MOV better than MP4 for QuickTime or Final Cut Pro?

Not automatically. MOV is useful when your app, editor, or delivery spec specifically asks for a QuickTime container. For broad sharing, MP4 is often the safer format.

### Can you just change .mp4 to .mov?

You can rename the file, but that does not convert it. If your app is strict about containers or timing metadata, a rename can still fail. Use a real transcode when you need a dependable MOV file.

### Why does your MPEG-4 file fail in QuickTime even though it is an MP4?

The extension is only one part of compatibility. QuickTime also depends on the codecs and track data inside the container. GrepCut exports H.264 video and AAC audio inside MOV to match a common QuickTime-friendly combination.

### Is there a file size limit for MPEG-4 to MOV conversion?

There is no server upload limit because your file is not sent to GrepCut. The practical limit is your browser, device memory, and processing power.

### Will the MOV file work on Windows?

It can work in Windows apps that support MOV plus H.264 and AAC. If you need the broadest playback compatibility, MP4 may be a better export target. Choose MOV when your workflow asks for QuickTime.
