# Free Online Video Tools | GrepCut

Free browser-based video tools - trim, crop, resize, mute and more. Processed entirely on your device.

HTML: https://grepcut.com/en/tools


## Highlights

### Private by default

Videos are processed on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

### No install, no sign-up

Open the page, drop a file, and edit it right away.

### Fast on supported browsers

WebCodecs keeps processing smooth and responsive where available.
