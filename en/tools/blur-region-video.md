# Blur Video Region Online

Protect privacy by blurring specific areas of your video clip. Processes entirely in your browser to guarantee 100% security.

HTML: https://grepcut.com/en/tools/blur-region-video

## How to Blur a Specific Area in Your Video Online

1. **Upload your video**: Choose an MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV file from your device and load it into the preview workspace.
2. **Place the blur box**: Drag and resize the rectangle over the face, license plate, document, password, logo, or screen area you want to hide.
3. **Adjust the blur strength**: Increase the blur when the text or face is still readable, or lower it when you only need a subtle privacy mask.
4. **Export the MP4**: Click Blur Region & Export to process the pixels locally in your browser and download the finished MP4.

This tool is best when the sensitive area stays in one place, such as a dashboard camera plate, a corner watermark, a fixed webcam face, or a document visible on a desk.

## When You Need One Clean Blur Box, Not a Full Video Editor

If you only need to hide one region, opening a full timeline editor can feel heavier than the job itself. You may need to cover a plate before sharing a driving clip, blur a name tag in a work recording, or hide a password that appears on screen for the whole take.

GrepCut keeps that workflow direct. You position one rectangular blur area on the preview, choose how strong the blur should be, and export a new MP4 with the edited video pixels.

Your file stays on your device while the tool works in the browser. That matters when the thing you are hiding is sensitive before the edit, not just after it.

### Use it for fixed or mostly fixed details

- **Faces in a static shot**: Cover a face in a webcam, interview, classroom, meeting, or CCTV-style clip when the face stays inside the same area.
- **License plates and addresses**: Hide a plate, street number, delivery label, invoice, or other private detail before you post or send the clip.
- **Screens and documents**: Blur a login field, private chat, spreadsheet cell, customer name, internal dashboard, or document corner visible in the recording.

If the subject moves across the frame, a static rectangle may not be enough. For moving faces or plates, use tracking in [GrepCut Studio](/) instead.

## Static Blur Box vs Tracking Blur

| Need | Use this tool when | Use tracking when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One fixed area | The sensitive detail stays inside one rectangle for the whole clip. | The detail moves across the screen or changes size. |
| Quick privacy edit | You want a fast browser workflow without building a timeline. | You need keyframes, masks, or per-scene adjustments. |
| Multiple faces | You only need to cover one group or one fixed zone. | You need separate blur areas for several moving faces. |
| Review effort | You can check one area once, then export. | You must scrub through the clip and confirm the blur follows the subject. |

A static blur box is simple because it does less. That is the advantage when your private detail is fixed, and the limitation when it moves.

## Why Local Processing Helps Before You Share

A privacy edit has a timing problem: the original video is sensitive before the blur is applied. If you upload the raw clip to a remote editor first, the unblurred version may already be outside your control.

With this browser-side tool, the frame processing happens locally. You can prepare a shareable version without sending the original file to GrepCut servers.

For stronger privacy, review the exported video before publishing it. A blur over the face or plate helps, but your clip may still reveal identity through voices, names spoken aloud, background signs, reflections, uniforms, or location clues.

## Blur Video Region Advantages

### Advantages

- Your video is processed locally in the browser, with no upload.
- You can drag and resize the blur box directly on the preview.
- You can change the blur strength before exporting.
- The tool is free and watermark-free.
- The audio track is preserved while the video pixels are edited.

### Disadvantages

- Only one rectangular blur region is applied.
- The blur area is static, so it will not follow a moving subject.
- The export is MP4, even if your input file uses another container.

## Blur Video Region FAQ

### Can you blur only part of a video?

Yes. You can place one rectangular blur box over the area you want to hide, adjust its size and strength, then export the edited MP4.

### Will your video be uploaded while you blur the region?

No. The processing runs locally in your browser, so your original file is not uploaded to GrepCut servers.

### Can you blur a moving face or license plate?

This tool uses a static box, so it works best when the face, plate, or private detail stays in one area. If the subject moves, open [GrepCut Studio](/) and use tracking instead.

### Can you blur multiple regions in the same video?

This landing page tool applies one rectangular blur region. If you need several blur zones, separate masks, or tracked objects, use [GrepCut Studio](/).

### Does the blur tool remove or change your audio?

No. The tool changes the video pixels in the selected region and preserves the audio track as-is.

### What video formats can you load?

You can load common video files such as MP4, MOV, WebM, and MKV. The processed result is exported as an MP4.

### How strong should the blur be?

Set the blur high enough that the face, plate, document, or screen text cannot be read in the preview. For highly sensitive footage, review the exported MP4 before you share it.

### Is blur always enough for privacy?

Blur can hide the selected pixels, but your video may still reveal information through voices, names, reflections, signs, GPS-like scenery, or other context. Check the whole frame before publishing.
