## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GrepCut an online video editor?

Yes, GrepCut is a fully browser-based AI video editor. You don't need to download or install any software. You can edit, render, and export your videos directly from your browser, securely and efficiently.

### How does Ai object tracking work?

Our Ai object tracking automatically follows objects or people frame-by-frame. You just select the subject you want to mask, and the AI algorithm intelligently tracks its movement throughout the video, saving you hours of manual rotoscoping.

### Can I generate subtitles automatically?

Absolutely. GrepCut features an AI-powered transcription tool that accurately generates subtitles for your videos in just one click. You can also edit and style them to match your branding.
